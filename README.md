### Hi, I'm Mike Hansky

London travel writer and co-founder of [London Tourist Map](https://londontouristmap.com/), a free interactive map of 400+ London sights, 17 themed maps, 17 day trips and 38 hidden gems.

- Built with Astro, MapLibre GL, OpenStreetMap and Protomaps, hosted on Cloudflare Pages
- Every place shows if it is open now, ticket prices, visit time, accessibility notes and the walk from the nearest station
- Free, no sign-up
